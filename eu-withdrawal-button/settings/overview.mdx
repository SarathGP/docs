## Overview

The **Settings** page is where you tailor the app to your store. The defaults are already EU-ready, so
you only need to visit this page if you want to change the button's look, adjust the cooling-off
period, or control how emails are sent.

Changes are saved using the save bar at the top of the page — make your edits, then click **Save**
(or **Discard** to undo).
