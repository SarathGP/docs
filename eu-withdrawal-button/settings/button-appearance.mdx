## Overview

The **Withdrawal button** section of Settings controls how the floating button looks and where it
appears on your storefront. A few small changes can make it blend right in with your brand.

## Options

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Position" icon="arrows-up-down-left-right">
    Where the button sits on the screen. Choose **Bottom right** (the default), **Bottom left**, or
    **Bottom center**. Pick a corner that won't clash with other widgets like chat bubbles.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Size" icon="up-right-and-down-left-from-center">
    How large the button is: **Small**, **Medium**, or **Large**. Larger sizes are easier to spot;
    smaller sizes are more subtle.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Background colour" icon="fill-drip">
    The button's fill colour. Defaults to black (`#1A1A1A`). Use the colour picker or type a hex code
    to match your brand.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Text colour" icon="font">
    The colour of the button's label. Defaults to white (`#FFFFFF`). Make sure it contrasts well with
    the background colour so the text stays readable.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Button text" icon="pen">
    The label shown on the button. Defaults to **"Withdraw from contract"**, automatically shown in
    your customer's language. Enter your own text (up to 80 characters) to override it, or clear the
    field to go back to the per-language defaults.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
Use your store's primary or accent colour for the background, and double-check the text colour stays
readable against it.
</Tip>

## Languages

The default button text and the customer-facing withdrawal flow are translated into several major EU
languages — English, German, Dutch, French, Italian, and Spanish — and shown automatically based on
the customer's language. If you set your own custom button text, that exact text is used for everyone.
