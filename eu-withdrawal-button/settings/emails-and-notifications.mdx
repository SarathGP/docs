## Overview

The app sends two kinds of email automatically: a **confirmation email** to the customer, and a
**notification email** to you. The **Email sender** and **Notifications** sections of Settings let you
control how these appear and where they're delivered.

## Who gets which email

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Confirmation email (to customer)" icon="envelope-circle-check">
    Sent when a customer confirms a withdrawal. It contains their declaration, the items, a reference
    number, and the timestamp. This email is the **durable legal record** of the withdrawal.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Notification email (to you)" icon="bell">
    Sent at the same time so you know a new withdrawal has arrived and can start processing it.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Email sender settings

These control how the confirmation email appears to your customers:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Sender name" icon="signature">
    The name customers see as the sender. Defaults to your shop's name. Up to 255 characters.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reply-to email" icon="reply">
    The address that receives replies if a customer responds to the confirmation email. Defaults to
    the shop owner's email.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Notification settings

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Notification email" icon="at">
    The address where withdrawal alerts are sent. We recommend setting this to whoever handles
    customer service or refunds. If you leave it blank, alerts fall back to your **reply-to email**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
Send notifications to a shared inbox (like `support@yourstore.com`) so withdrawals aren't missed if
one person is away.
</Tip>

## If a confirmation email fails

Occasionally an email can't be delivered (for example, a mistyped address). When this happens:

- The customer still sees their on-screen receipt and can **print or save** it.
- The withdrawal's [compliance status](/eu-withdrawal-button/managing/withdrawal-detail#compliance-status)
  shows **Confirmation email failed**.
- You can open the request and click **Resend confirmation email** to try again.
