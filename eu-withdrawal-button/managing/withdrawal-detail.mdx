## Overview

Open any request (from the dashboard or the [withdrawals list](/eu-withdrawal-button/managing/withdrawals-list))
to see its full details. This is where you review what the customer asked for, track where you are
with it, and — if needed — resend the confirmation email.

## What's on the detail page

The page is organized into clear sections:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Order" icon="receipt">
    The order number (a link to the order in Shopify), the total amount, and the currency.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Customer" icon="user">
    The customer's name (a link to their Shopify profile), email, language, and country. If the
    customer's data has been removed for GDPR reasons, that's noted here.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Items withdrawn" icon="boxes-stacked">
    A table of the products being withdrawn — image, title, options, the quantity withdrawn versus the
    quantity ordered, and the line price.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reason" icon="comment">
    The reason the customer gave, if they chose to provide one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Event timeline" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    A complete, time-stamped log of everything that happened — declaration submitted, confirmation
    submitted, emails sent or failed, status changes, and more. This is your audit trail.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

At the top of the page you'll also find a **Resend confirmation email** button, useful if the
original email didn't reach the customer.

## Understanding the two statuses

Each withdrawal has **two** kinds of status. It's important to understand the difference.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Compliance status" icon="shield-check">
    Set **automatically** by the app. It reflects where the customer is in the legal withdrawal
    process — you don't change this yourself.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Processing status" icon="person-running">
    Set **by you**. It tracks your own internal handling of the request — separate from the legal
    process.
  </Card>
</Columns>

### Compliance status

This is the app's automatic record of the request's progress:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Declared** | The customer started the request but hasn't confirmed yet. |
| **Confirmed** | The customer confirmed the withdrawal. |
| **Confirmation sent** | The confirmation email was delivered successfully — the legally binding record. |
| **Confirmation email failed** | The email couldn't be delivered. Use **Resend confirmation email** to try again. |
| **Abandoned** | The request was started but never completed. |
| **Redacted (GDPR)** | The customer's personal data was removed following a GDPR request. |

### Processing status

This is your own tracking of where you are with the request. Choose a status from the dropdown and
click **Save status**:

| Status | Use it when… |
| --- | --- |
| **In progress** | You're reviewing or handling the request (this is the default). |
| **Completed** | You've finished — for example, you've issued the refund. |
| **Rejected** | You've declined the request, e.g. it turned out to be out of scope. |

<Tip>
The **Pending** and **Completed** counts on the home dashboard are based on the processing status —
so keeping these up to date keeps your dashboard accurate.
</Tip>

## A typical processing flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the request">
    Click into the withdrawal from the dashboard or list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the details">
    Check the items, quantities, value, and reason. Confirm the compliance status shows
    *Confirmation sent*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Issue any refund in Shopify">
    Use the linked order to process a refund through Shopify as you normally would.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the processing status">
    Set the status to **Completed** (or **Rejected** if you're declining) and click **Save status**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

correctly. Keep these records in case you ever need to demonstrate compliance.
