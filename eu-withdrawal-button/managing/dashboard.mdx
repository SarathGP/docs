## Overview

The home dashboard is the first page you see when you open the app. It gives you a quick summary of
your withdrawal activity and a shortcut to the most recent requests.

## What's on the dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Setup Guide" icon="list-check">
    When the app isn't fully set up, a guide at the top shows the remaining step — enabling the
    withdrawal button in your theme. Once you've done that, the guide marks itself complete. See the
    [Quickstart](/eu-withdrawal-button/quickstart) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dashboard metrics" icon="chart-simple">
    A set of numbers summarizing your withdrawals at a glance:

    | Metric | Meaning |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Total requests** | Every withdrawal request received. |
    | **Pending** | Requests still waiting for you to process. |
    | **Completed** | Requests you've marked as completed. |
    | **Verified** | Requests where the confirmation email was sent successfully. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recent withdrawals" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    A short table of the **5 most recent** requests, showing the reference number, order, customer,
    status, amount, country, and date. Click any row to open its full details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Support" icon="life-ring">
    A help card with links to documentation and support if you need a hand.
  </Step>
</Steps>
