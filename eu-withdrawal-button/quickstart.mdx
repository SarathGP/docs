## Overview

Getting started takes just a few minutes. You'll install the app, then turn on the withdrawal button
in your theme. There is only **one required setup step** after installation — the app guides you
through it.

## Step 1: Install from the Shopify App Store

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the app">
    Go to the [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/) and search for **Clevyre EU Withdrawal
    Button**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the app">
    Click **Add app** to install it on your store. This is the same process as any other Shopify app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve permissions">
    Shopify will ask you to approve the access the app needs (such as reading your orders and
    customers, and adding a block to your theme). Review it and click **Install**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After installing, you land on the app's home dashboard inside your Shopify admin.

## Step 2: Enable the withdrawal button in your theme

When you first open the app, the home dashboard shows a **Setup Guide** with one step:
*"Enable the withdrawal footer link in your theme."*

The button won't appear on your storefront until you complete this step.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the theme editor">
    On the home dashboard, click **Open theme editor** in the Setup Guide card. This opens Shopify's
    theme editor with the **Clevyre EU Withdrawal Button** block ready to add.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the block">
    Add the withdrawal button block to your theme (the footer is a good place). The block makes the
    floating button appear on every storefront page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** in the top-right corner of the theme editor to apply the change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Once the block is enabled, return to the app's home dashboard — it automatically detects that the
step is complete and marks the Setup Guide as done.
</Tip>

## Step 3: Confirm the button is live

Visit your storefront. You should see the floating **"Withdraw from contract"** button in the corner
you chose (the bottom-right by default). Clicking it opens the customer withdrawal page.

<Check>
That's it — your store now offers a compliant withdrawal process. The defaults are EU-ready out of
the box (a 14-day withdrawal window and the legally required wording).
</Check>
